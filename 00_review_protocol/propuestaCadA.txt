TITLE: Measurement instruments for technical and transversal competencies in engineering education: a systematic review of measurement properties

DOMAIN: engineering_competency_measurement

REVIEW_TYPE: systematic

N_AGENTS: 3

TOPIC_STATEMENT:
This review examines human-measurement instruments used with engineering students to assess technical or transversal competencies and the empirical evidence supporting their measurement properties.

REVIEW_QUESTION:
Which instruments were developed, validated, or culturally adapted from 2014 through 2026 to measure technical or transversal competencies in undergraduate or postgraduate engineering students, and what empirical measurement-property evidence do they report?

QUESTION_FRAMEWORK:
TYPE: PCC
POPULATION: Undergraduate or postgraduate engineering students, including mixed samples in which engineering students are explicitly represented.
CONCEPT: The eligible competency-measurement construct as defined in SCOPE_OF_REVIEW.
CONTEXT: Engineering education in higher-education institutions.

OBJECTIVE:
Identify and characterize instruments for measuring technical and transversal competencies in engineering students and map the empirical evidence for their measurement properties for subsequent COSMIN-adapted appraisal.

SCOPE_OF_REVIEW:
The review covers empirical human-measurement studies published from 2014 through 2026 in English, Spanish, or Portuguese, involving undergraduate or postgraduate engineering students and intended to measure a declared technical or transversal competency.
CORE CONSTRUCT: An eligible competency-measurement study develops, validates, or culturally adapts a questionnaire, scale, inventory, rubric, situational test, self-report measure, or performance-based measure and provides original empirical evidence for at least one measurement property in the target sample. The positive discriminator is the conjunction of (a) a declared engineering competency measured in people, (b) an identifiable measurement instrument, and (c) original measurement-property analysis such as content validity, structural validity, internal consistency, inter-rater reliability, test-retest reliability, construct or criterion validity, measurement invariance, or item-response analysis. Technical competencies include design, simulation, programming, technical problem solving, and prototyping; transversal competencies include critical thinking, collaboration, communication, ethics, self-regulation, employability skills, and domain self-efficacy.
NOT an eligible competency-measurement study by itself (near-misses): use of an established instrument solely as an intervention outcome; course grades, GPA, ordinary examinations, satisfaction surveys, or learning assessments presented only for grading; physical instruments, sensors, signal measurement, test benches, dimensional scales, or engineering instrumentation; validation of circuits, algorithms, simulations, computational models, or teaching systems; personality traits not declared as professional competencies; competence measures applied exclusively to teachers, school pupils, non-student professionals, or non-engineering students; AI used merely as a teaching tool while the measured outcome is unrelated to an engineering competency.
CONSTRUCT SEVERITY: STRICT -> the abstract explicitly confirms the declared engineering competency, the identifiable human-measurement instrument, the relevant engineering-student context, and original empirical analysis of at least one measurement property: INCLUDE. MODERATE -> the abstract claims instrument development, validation, adaptation, reliability, validity, or rubric calibration but leaves the exact property, engineering composition of a mixed sample, or instrument role partial or ambiguous: UNCERTAIN, never exclude. NEAR_MISS -> the abstract explicitly states one of the near-miss designs and thereby establishes that the object assessed is not the core construct: EXCLUDE.

ELIGIBLE_DOCS: original_research_article; early_access_original_article; indexed_conference_paper_with_complete_psychometric_evidence

INELIGIBLE_DOCS: review_article; systematic_review; scoping_review; narrative_review; editorial; commentary; book_chapter; thesis; dissertation; preprint; protocol; abstract_only_record

DOCUMENT_RULE:
Apply this hard gate before thematic relevance. Eligible records are peer-reviewed original research articles, early-access original articles, and indexed conference papers whose title and abstract confirm a complete empirical psychometric study. Ineligible records are reviews, editorials, commentaries, book chapters, theses, dissertations, preprints, protocols, and abstract-only records. Document type is judged only here. If indexed-conference status or completeness cannot be established from the available metadata and abstract, route the record to UNCERTAIN.

INCLUSION_CRITERIA:
- The record passes the document-type gate per DOCUMENT_RULE.
- The study satisfies the core construct as defined in SCOPE_OF_REVIEW.
- The measured construct concerns a declared technical or transversal competency relevant to engineering education.
- The empirical population includes undergraduate or postgraduate engineering students; a mixed population qualifies when engineering students are explicitly represented.
- The record reports original empirical evidence for at least one measurement property of the instrument.
- The publication year is 2014 through 2026 inclusive and the publication language is English, Spanish, or Portuguese.

EXCLUSION_CRITERIA:
- The record is positively identified as ineligible per DOCUMENT_RULE.
- The abstract explicitly identifies the measured object as a near-miss defined in SCOPE_OF_REVIEW.
- The abstract explicitly states that the study only administers an established measure to estimate an intervention effect, association, perception, or outcome.
- The abstract explicitly limits participants to school pupils, teachers, non-student professionals, or students outside engineering.
- The abstract explicitly describes the outcome as course achievement, grades, GPA, a content examination, satisfaction, or performance of a technical artifact rather than a declared competency construct.
- The year or language metadata explicitly falls outside the stated scope.

AGENT_INCLUDE_WHEN:
- After confirming DOCUMENT_RULE, locate a purpose verb such as develop, validate, adapt, translate, calibrate, or evaluate a measurement model and link it to a named human-measurement instrument.
- Locate an explicit engineering-student population span and an explicit competency label, then verify that both refer to the same empirical sample.
- Locate at least one reported measurement-property method or result, such as expert content appraisal, factor analysis, alpha or omega, inter-rater agreement, test-retest, invariance, item-response analysis, criterion relation, or discriminant evidence.
- Treat a mixed intervention-and-validation study as includable when the abstract separately reports original instrument-development or measurement-property results.
- Treat a translated or culturally adapted instrument as original measurement-property evidence when the abstract reports empirical evaluation in the new language or setting.

AGENT_EXCLUDE_WHEN:
- Metadata or the abstract explicitly matches an ineligible category per DOCUMENT_RULE.
- The abstract's measured entity is explicitly a device, signal, circuit, algorithm, simulation, computational model, educational platform, or other technical artifact.
- The methods explicitly say an established questionnaire or scale was administered only to compare groups, predict outcomes, or estimate an intervention effect, and all reported results concern those substantive outcomes.
- The participants are explicitly restricted to an out-of-scope population defined in SCOPE_OF_REVIEW.
- The assessment is explicitly a course test, grade, GPA, satisfaction rating, or ordinary project evaluation used to score learning rather than measure a declared competency construct.

UNCERTAINTY_HANDLING:
This precedence rule overrides the exclusion criteria whenever confirming information is absent, incomplete, or ambiguous. Confirm three axes: construct (the core construct as defined in SCOPE_OF_REVIEW), context (engineering-student population and eligible year/language), and evidence (original empirical measurement-property analysis); also confirm the document gate per DOCUMENT_RULE. If an axis is merely unconfirmed from title, abstract, keywords, or metadata, assign UNCERTAIN for human or full-text review and never EXCLUDE. Exclude only when the record positively asserts a disqualifying fact.

JUSTIFICATION_STYLE: Concise, evidence-based rationales grounded only in title, abstract, keywords and supplied metadata; name the decisive axis (document type, construct, context, or evidence) and quote the shortest span that settled the vote.

CALIBRATION_ANCHORS:
